Multi-Armed Bandits

Bandits isolate one RL core tension: do you exploit your current best arm, or explore uncertain options?

Lesson map

Intuition

If you over-exploit early, you can lock onto a mediocre arm. Exploration is a deliberate cost paid for better estimates.

Formalism

ε-greedy policy: choose random arm with probability ε, else choose arm with highest estimated value.

Action rule: a_t = random with ε, otherwise argmax_a Q_t(a).

Worked Example

With true means [0.22, 0.52, 0.74], arm C is optimal but unknown. Early random pulls help discover C.

Common Mistakes

  • Keeping ε fixed forever (no annealing).
  • Comparing algorithms with too few pulls.
  • Ignoring non-stationary rewards.
