Core Theory

Markov Decision Process + Bellman Intuition

MDP = (S, A, P, R, γ). Markov means future depends only on current state and action (not full history).

Bellman Optimality (easy words)

Best value at a state = best action today + discounted best future value tomorrow.

V*(s) = max_a Σ P(s'|s,a) [R(s,a,s') + γV*(s')]

Think: choose action that gives strong immediate reward and leads to better future states.
