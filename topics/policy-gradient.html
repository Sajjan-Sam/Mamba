Policy Gradient

Instead of estimating action values first, optimize the policy parameters directly by ascending expected return.

Intuition

Increase probability of actions that led to good outcomes; reduce probability of poor actions. This works well for continuous-control and stochastic policies.

Common Mistakes

  • High variance from short rollouts without baseline.
  • Learning rates that collapse policy entropy too quickly.
  • Ignoring reward normalization.
