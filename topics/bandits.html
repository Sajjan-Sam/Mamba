A bandit has many slot-machine arms. Each arm has unknown payout. You must balance exploration and exploitation.

How epsilon-greedy works

  • With probability ε, try random arm (explore).
  • With probability 1-ε, choose best-known arm (exploit).
  • Update average reward estimate after each pull.
