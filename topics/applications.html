Where Reinforcement Learning Creates Value

This final page connects algorithms to practical outcomes so learners know when to choose RL and when not to.

Robotics

Manipulation, locomotion, and adaptive control. Use simulation-first, then transfer carefully to real hardware.

Logistics

Route optimization, dynamic dispatching, and warehouse throughput improvement under changing demand.

Recommendations

Long-term user satisfaction optimization, not only immediate clicks.

Practical checklist

  • Define clear reward without loopholes.
  • Build a safe simulator and evaluate edge cases.
  • Track sample efficiency and policy stability.
  • Add human oversight for high-risk decisions.

References for learning direction: common RL syllabi from Stanford and structured RL notes/books in the style of Shiyu Zhao's educational material.
