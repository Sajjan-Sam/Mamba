Markov Decision Process + Bellman Thinking

MDPs define the structure; Bellman equations define the recursive logic for optimal behavior.

Intuition

“Markov” means present state is a sufficient summary. You do not need the full history if state representation is complete.

Formalism

MDP tuple: (S, A, P, R, γ). Optimal value follows Bellman optimality.

V*(s) = max_a Σ_{s'} P(s'|s,a)[R(s,a,s') + γV*(s')].

Worked Example

In gridworld, moving toward the goal may require temporarily moving away from immediate reward to avoid traps.

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming Markov property holds without enough state features.
  • Ignoring transition uncertainty in planning.
  • Confusing policy evaluation with policy improvement.
